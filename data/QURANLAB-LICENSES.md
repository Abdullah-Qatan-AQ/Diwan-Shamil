## Included Arabic configurations

The included configurations are the six books, Muwatta Malik, Musnad Ahmad, Sunan al-Darimi, the Forty Hadith of al-Nawawi, Forty Hadith Qudsi, and Forty Hadith of Shah Wali Allah al-Dihlawi.

## Open-Hadith-Data layer

The Arabic matn for the included configurations is distributed with the source notices supplied by QuranLab under **ODbL 1.0** for the database and **DbCL 1.0** for the contents. Keep the attribution, source notices, license URI, and any required share-alike notice when redistributing the database or a derivative database.

## Integrity and removal

QuranLab states that grades are attributed to their graders and are not assertions by QuranLab. Do not present a stored grade as an independent verification by this application. Removal or correction requests should be directed to the QuranLab dataset Community tab.

The MIT license in the repository root applies to the application code only. It does **not** relicense the included hadith data.
